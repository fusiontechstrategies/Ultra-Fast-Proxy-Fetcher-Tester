# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and technically honest.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, source policies, and responsible-use boundaries.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help maintain a safe project environment.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, live proxy endpoints, or sensitive operational data.
- Encouraging unlawful, unauthorized, deceptive, or abusive activity.
- Disrupting project spaces or repeatedly ignoring maintainer direction.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially and fairly as practical.
